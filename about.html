About the Application

Road Damage Detector is a web-based application designed to help identify, report, and manage road damage efficiently. Our system utilizes advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze images of road surfaces and automatically detect various types of damage including potholes, cracks, rutting, and edge damage.

The application provides a user-friendly interface for reporting road issues, visualizing damage locations on an interactive map, and tracking the status of reported problems. Municipalities and road maintenance agencies can use this tool to prioritize repairs and improve road safety.
